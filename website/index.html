A quick look at how News Widgets feels on a real macOS desktop.

Four trackers. One install.

The same app bundle ships four native widgets. Pick any one, or run all four side by side. Each lives in its own GitHub repo.

News

AI Today + World news from the sources you pick. Real photos, real summaries, refreshed all day.

See it → bendawg2010/NewsWidgets

Scores

Live NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, and EPL scores. Tap a game to open the matchup page in your browser.

Open Sports → bendawg2010/Scores

F1 Live

Live driver positions, lap times, gap to leader. Powered by OpenF1.

Open F1 Live → bendawg2010/F1Live

Sports News

Top sports headlines from ESPN, Fox Sports, BBC Sport, and CBS Sports. Tap to read.

Open Sports News → bendawg2010/SportsNews

Plus five free apps.

Side-projects you can use today. Web apps, bookmarklets, and a desktop widget. No accounts, no ads. Each lives in its own GitHub repo.

ClassSchedule

Drop in your school timetable, get smart "next class" alerts and a clean weekly view. Local-first, no signup.

Open ClassSchedule → bendawg2010/ClassSchedule

StudyDeck

Quizlet, but free. Five game modes — Flashcards, Match, Falling Blocks, Block Blast, Test. Zero ads, zero accounts.

Open StudyDeck → bendawg2010/StudyDeck

DeckGrab

One-click bookmarklet that yanks any Quizlet set into plain text. Exports to TSV, CSV, JSON, Anki, or StudyDeck.

AssignmentTracker

Stay ahead of every due date. Dashboard, calendar, smart sort, late warnings, weighted grades. Local-first, no signup.

Open AssignmentTracker → bendawg2010/AssignmentTracker

DueIn

Native macOS countdown widget. Graduation in 47 days. AP Bio in 14. Hawaii in 120. On your desktop, free, open source.

Open DueIn → bendawg2010/DueIn
